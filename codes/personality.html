Top Personality Traits, Hobbies & Academic Impact

  • Extroversion & Networking: Being naturally extroverted helps me get out there to build connections. Networking isn't just a great way to meet people—it opens doors to study groups and friendships with peers whose stories I would never have heard otherwise.
  • Openness to Experience & Enthusiasm: Having an openness to experience gives a taste for fresh, new things. Trying unfamiliar challenges leaves unforgettable lessons and helps me approach difficult coursework with curiosity. Keeping an enthusiastic attitude keeps motivation high, because a downcast mindset only makes challenges harder.
  • Hobbies & Rest: Enjoying hobbies like reading, writing, sleeping, gaming, and watching anime keeps me balanced. Though sleep habits might occasionally tempt a bit of procrastination, balancing rest with my studies keeps me grounded.

My Hero / Mentor

Background & Why She Is My Hero: My sister is the person I look up to the most. She has always been someone who takes action when others hesitate, embodying confidence and a wonderful presence. What makes her my hero isn't a cape or superhuman strength, but her incredible courage and resilience. Faced with obstacles that would have me overwhelmed, she puts on a brave face and keeps pushing forward.

Favorite Quote & Impact: Her guiding philosophy is captured in her quote: "Everyone dies, but as long as you remember them, did they truly die?" She paved the way for our family and once advised me, "Don't be like me—be yourself," a piece of wisdom I carry with me everywhere.

Critical Essay: LLMs as a Tool, Not a Crutch

The statement "LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch" is a crucial reminder for modern students. Relying entirely on AI to write code or complete assignments prevents the development of authentic problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

When, Where, and How to Use LLMs: LLMs are best utilized as interactive tutors during coding and studying. They can be leveraged when debugging error messages, explaining dense technical documentation, or brainstorming syntax strategies to enhance understanding rather than bypassing the learning process.
